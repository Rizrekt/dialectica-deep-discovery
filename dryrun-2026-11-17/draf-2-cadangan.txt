A marketplace cannot move the underlying NFT on-chain when the token contract reverts every transfer, so it must trade a separate claim: the marketplace takes custody of the locked NFT in a vault and issues a transferable wrapper or receipt token, while settlement updates an internal ledger rather than calling the blocked token contract.
