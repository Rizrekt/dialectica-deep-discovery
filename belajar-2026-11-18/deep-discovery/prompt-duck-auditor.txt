THE SYSTEM (an autonomous agent answering expert questions on the Dialectica platform, earning $TRUED points):

Pipeline: A long-running "serve" poller (vendor CLI) fetches questions in a 2–6 hour age band → a doctrine layer classifies each question into lanes (late-entry vs fast-entry) from live answer-arrival rates → a writer model (GLM, via a relay provider, with a 2-step fallback chain ending at a free Cloudflare-hosted open model with a small daily quota) drafts a long cited answer → four gates check the draft before submission: (1) mechanical citation verification (do the cited identifiers actually resolve), (2) two-stage content verification (do precise claims appear in fetched sources + an entailment check by a judge model), (3) a chained semantic checker (primary judge model on its own lane; backup judge via a shared relay; a frontier tiebreaker via the same relay on disputes, 2-of-3), (4) a freshness gate re-checked at send time (crowd size and already-verified counts) → submission via the vendor CLI → verdicts arrive hours-to-days later from verifier panels; only novelty (≥15% new content) and marathon-completion achievements pay (+10 each); ordinary verified answers pay 0; falsified answers cost reputation/suspensions, not coins.

Measured facts: 5 pilot answers → 4 paid (3× +20, 1× +10). Two of the paid answers had fabricated citation apparatus that the operator's own spot-check caught but panels still paid. The writer model's strength is content/structure; its weakness is inventing precise pointers (DOIs, article numbers). The primary judge catches 7/7 known-fatal drafts; relay judges 5–6/7. One full batch burned 9 generations with 0 submissions because gates (correctly) held every draft, and one dropped claim cost a reputation slash. A smoke test proved the doctrine pool seam works (48/48 served rows on-doctrine) but the only draft that reached generation died because the fallback provider's daily free quota had been burned earlier that day by calibration tests. The account's concurrency is 1; a dropped job locks the slot ~30 min with no release API. The operator (an AI orchestrator) runs everything; the human owner approves paid/risky/irreversible steps only. Verdict monitoring is manual (API polls), no automated watcher. There is a second, unbuilt lane: working as a Verifier (judging others' answers, ~0.8 points per verdict at high volume) — kit built, never registered.
