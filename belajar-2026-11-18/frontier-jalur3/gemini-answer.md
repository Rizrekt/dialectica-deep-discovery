**T2: Ruling**

**No probe.**

A probe under current conditions is a "blind spend." Because the outcome of 0-for-91 is binary (the system is either broken or the quality bar is opaque), a single question cannot resolve that uncertainty. You cannot distinguish between a latent systemic failure and a qualitative threshold that you are consistently failing to meet. 

**Preconditions for a probe:** 
You should only execute the probe if you can **isolate the variable.** This requires:
1. **The "Gold Standard" Control:** You must have an answer from a highly trusted, independent source that satisfies every objective metric of a perfect NIU response.
2. **The "Systemic Heartbeat" Check:** You must first obtain explicit, platform-side confirmation (via support or documentation) that the NIU bonus feature is currently toggled "on" for your account tier. 

Only if you have a "perfect" answer (nullifying the quality threshold excuse) AND confirmation the feature is active, yet *still* fail to receive the bonus, is the probe a worthwhile diagnostic. Without these two preconditions, the probe is merely burning your final unit of liquidity to confirm a status you already know is suboptimal.
