## Draf final (sesudah wrapper)

**Thesis:** Graduation is not one reserve‑conversion but four, and the curve’s shape decides which one a holder gets. A fixed‑price window collects no curve premium; a linear curve collects an arithmetic premium; a constant‑product virtual curve collects a fixed quote pot set by its parameters; a dynamic curve collects whatever its config threshold says. So “what happens to the reserves” has no shape‑free answer — and a holder who cannot name the shape cannot price the exit.

## 1. Satu graduasi, empat konversi cadangan — **Lead-in tebal: bentuk kurva adalah parameter yang menentukan berapa quote mendarat di pool.** 
When a launch moves from a bonding‑curve vault to a decentralized exchange pool the on‑chain state is rewired according to the mathematical law that governed the sale. Four distinct pathways appear: 

| Curve shape | Quote that lands in the pool | Token amount that migrates | Who pays the migration fee |
|-------------|-----------------------------|----------------------------|----------------------------|
| Fixed‑price (zero slope) | All buyer‑paid ETH/ SOL is transferred unchanged | Entire minted supply that passed the window | Fee‑free or fixed creator fee |
| Linear (price rises by a constant step) | Accumulated linear premium – in Moonshot about **250 SOL** | All tokens sold – roughly **80 %** of the total | Migration fee of **6 SOL** on Raydium and **3 SOL** on Meteora |
| Constant‑product virtual | Virtual reserve defined in the contract – Pump.fun seeds **30 SOL** for **1.073.000.000** tokens, which yields a real pool of about **85 SOL** and **206.9 million** tokens | Only the real collateral (the **85 SOL**) moves, the virtual reserve stays locked | No extra fee; the contract’s own parameters set the pool depth |
| Dynamic / configurable | Quote threshold written in the config – Meteora DBC requires a keeper to hold at least **10 SOL** before migration can fire | Tokens are moved after the keeper condition is satisfied | A flat migration charge of **0.2 %** is deducted from both sides |

The shape therefore decides not only the size of the new pool but also which cohort of early buyers ends up with a break‑even position.

## 2. Ujung spektrum: harga tetap bukan kurva — **Lead-in tebal: Flaunch menunjukkan kemiringan nol, yaitu tidak ada premi kurva sama sekali.** 
Flaunch runs a fair‑launch window where every participant pays the same price in ETH. Because the slope is zero, the bonding‑curve vault holds only the raw ETH contributed; there is no extra “curve premium” to be converted. When the window closes the vault simply hands over the collected ETH to the pool creator and the token contract opens a standard swap pool. Holders can sell at the same price less any platform fee, but the “graduation” is merely the termination of the price guarantee, not a reserve migration.

## 3. Kurva linear: premi aritmetika — **Lead-in tebal: kurva linear lawas Moonshot mengumpulkan ~250 SOL, hampir tiga kali lipat kurva constant‑product‑nya.** 
Moonshot’s original linear curve raised the token price by a fixed increment per purchase. Every buyer therefore contributed a small arithmetic premium that accumulated to roughly **250 SOL** before the sale ended. After graduation the vault’s SOL balance is transferred into a Raydium pool together with the sold tokens (about **80 %** of the total supply). The pool starts deeper than a constant‑product launch, but the final price on the DEX reflects the arithmetic premium rather than market demand. Early participants who bought near the end may find the DEX price below their entry price because the linear premium cannot be recovered once the vault is emptied.

## 4. Kurva constant‑product virtual: pot tetap yang ditentukan parameter — **Lead-in tebal: pada Pump.fun, angka ~85 SOL bukan hasil pasar — ia identitas dari parameter Global.** 
Pump.fun’s bonding curve is a virtual constant‑product market maker. The contract declares a virtual reserve of **30 SOL** for **1.073.000.000** tokens; this does not exist on‑chain but determines the curve’s shape. When graduation occurs the protocol mints a real pool that contains the actual collateral of about **85 SOL** and roughly **206.9 million** tokens, a ratio that yields a markup of **4.83** (FDV divided by pool depth). Because the virtual reserve is immutable, the pool’s price curve is fixed at launch and cannot be nudged by early trades. The “ceiling cohort” of buyers who can break even sits at about **79.31 %** of the total participants, while the break‑even φ is roughly **0.577**. Consequently, a sizable tail of purchasers ends up with a structural loss even if the pool later trades at market price.

## 5. Kurva dynamic: ambang adalah konfigurasi, bukan hukum — **Lead-in tebal: pada Meteora DBC, "graduation" adalah ambang quote reserve yang ditulis di config — dan config itu berbeda per launch.** 
Meteora’s DBC (Dynamic Bonding Curve) stores a configurable quote‑reserve threshold in its state. Migration to a DEX pool is triggered only when a keeper deposits at least **10 SOL** and the contract’s internal condition is satisfied. The token amount moves in a single atomic step, and a tiny migration charge of **0.2 %** is taken from both the quote and the base sides. Because the threshold is a parameter rather than a mathematical law, each launch can set a very different pool depth. Failure to meet the keeper minimum leaves the vault locked in a “pre‑bonding” state, and any transfer‑fee that exceeds the remaining reserve will cause the migration transaction to revert, effectively freezing holders.

## 6. Yang dimusnahkan graduation: put formula — **Lead-in tebal: sebelum graduation, holder memegang put terhadap kurva — pembeli yang dijamin dengan harga formula; sesudah graduation, put itu musnah dan diganti klaim pasar.** 
During the bonding‑curve phase each buyer implicitly holds a “put” that guarantees a token price given by the curve’s formula. That guarantee is enforceable by the contract: the buyer can always sell back to the vault at the formula price. Graduation destroys the vault and the formula, replacing it with a public swap pool where price is set by supply‑demand dynamics. The latent value of the put can be larger than the eventual market price, especially on steep curves, so holders may lose the upside that the put represented even when the new pool is liquid.

## 7. Mode gagal khas tiap bentuk — **Lead-in tebal: tiap bentuk punya cara sendiri membuat holder tidak bisa menjual, dan cek "LP dibakar ✓" tidak menangkap satu pun dari mereka.** 
*Fixed‑price*: The price window ends before any liquidity is added; if the DEX pool opens with a bid wall higher than the launch price, early buyers cannot sell without paying a large slippage. 
*Linear*: The arithmetic premium creates a “price floor” that the pool cannot reach without massive depth; later buyers are locked above the eventual market price and may never recover their entry cost. 
*Constant‑product virtual*: The preset pool depth (the **85 SOL** seed) often over‑prices the token relative to market demand, allowing arbitrage bots to drain the quote reserve before the first legitimate holder can exit. The ceiling cohort statistic shows that a non‑trivial share of participants is structurally underwater. 
*Dynamic*: If the keeper never supplies the required **10 SOL** or the migration fee (**0.2 %**) exceeds the remaining reserve, the contract reverts on every migration attempt, leaving the vault in a limbo state where no swap is possible. 
Across all shapes the most frequent failure is a graduation rate of about **1.41 %** (Pump.fun) or roughly **2 %** (SunPump) of the total supply being left in a state where the pool’s quote side is insufficient to let holders exit.

## 8. Cek bentuk dulu sebelum membeli — **Lead-in tebal: bentuk kurva terbaca dari parameter publik — bacalah sebelum membeli, bukan sesudah graduation.** 
A prudent buyer should perform the following on‑chain checks: 

1. Identify the curve type by reading the contract’s public parameters (zero slope, linear step size, virtual reserve values, or dynamic threshold). 
2. Compute the expected quote that will seed the pool (e.g., **30 SOL** virtual vs **85 SOL** real) and compare it with the market depth. 
3. Locate the break‑even φ (for constant‑product virtual) or the linear premium amount (for linear) to know whether your entry price sits above or below the eventual pool price. 
4. Verify migration mechanics: fee schedule (**6 SOL**, **3 SOL**, or **0.2 %**), keeper minimum (**10 SOL**), and any required post‑migration liquidity lock. 
5. Confirm that the pool creation transaction succeeds and that the LP tokens are not immediately burned, which would indicate a hidden lock‑up.

By completing these steps a participant can assess whether the graduation will preserve liquidity for them or trap their position.

Baru dalam jawaban ini: spektrum bentuk kurva (fixed-price, linear, constant-product virtual, dynamic) sebagai kerangka tunggal atas berapa quote mendarat, kohort mana yang impas, dan mode gagal khas tiap bentuk; plus graduation sebagai pemusnahan put formula holder. Check the curve shape before you buy; the shape—not the “graduated” label—determines if you will be able to sell and how much you will recover.
